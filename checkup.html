One polite, read-only checkup: certificate, uptime, mobile rendering, broken contact links, publicly exposed files.

What we check, and why

Certificate

An expired or broken certificate shows every visitor a full-page browser warning.

Uptime & errors

A site that answers with server errors is quietly turning customers away.

Mobile

Most visitors arrive on a phone. Without a viewport tag, your site renders zoomed out.

Contact links

We click your contact and booking links. A dead one means customers can't reach you at all.

Exposed files

Leftover config or source-control files can hand strangers your credentials. We look, we never store contents.

Security headers

Two standard headers that cost nothing to add and block whole classes of attacks.

Fair questions

Is this safe to run against my site?

The checkup sends at most about six ordinary page requests, the same as someone clicking around your homepage. No login attempts, no fuzzing, nothing written. It's a read-only visit by a very polite robot.

Do you store my results?

No. The report exists in your browser and in memory on our server just long enough to serve it to you, then it's gone. Nothing is shared with anyone.

You found problems. Now what?

Every finding links to what it means. If you want them fixed rather than explained, Patchhound ships most fixes same-day for a flat fee. Reply to any email from us, or write hello@patchhound.dev.

Why do you need the ownership checkbox?

So the tool can't be used to probe sites you don't represent. We also rate-limit scans per visitor.
